About Me

My professional journey in web development began with WordPress in 2018, with a pivotal breakthrough at Glorify, an e-commerce design platform. As a key developer, I was instrumental in transforming Glorify into a powerful tool that enables entrepreneurs to create professional visuals quickly and efficiently.

Glorify offers unique features like background removal, infinite canvas, and e-commerce-optimized design templates. The platform grew to over 300,000 users across 100 countries, generated $2 million in annual recurring revenue, and was featured on ProductHunt and in publications like Forbes and Entrepreneur.

After Glorify, I transitioned to freelancing, earning a top-rated badge on Upwork before working directly with clients. This journey led me to found Boson Studio, a software house designed to deliver innovative technological solutions. My approach centers on rapid skill acquisition, creative problem-solving, and developing practical web applications that address real-world business challenges.
